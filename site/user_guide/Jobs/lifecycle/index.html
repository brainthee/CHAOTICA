    Lifecycle¶

    Warning

    This page will be populated with more documentation soon.

    A job has a status which follows a strict workflow. Much of the functionality within CHAOTICA for a job is tied to it's status.

    What happens to phases¶

    Some job transitions cascade to the job's phases:

    • Lost: every phase that isn't finished yet (anything before Delivered, and not already cancelled, postponed, deleted or archived) is moved to Cancelled, and its scheduled timeslots are deleted. Delivered phases are left alone. The confirmation modal shows how many phases and slots will be cleared.
    • Deleted: every phase is moved to Deleted and its timeslots are removed. Phases that are already deleted don't block deleting the job.
    • Completed: happens automatically once all phases are finished, either when the last phase is delivered or via the background workflow task. Delivered, Cancelled, Deleted and Archived phases count as finished. A Postponed phase does not, so a job with a postponed phase stays In Progress. Completing a job by hand applies the same rule: it's refused while any phase is unfinished.

    Reopening a completed job¶

    Completed jobs show a Reopen Job button (anyone who can update the job). It moves the job back to Scoping and asks for a reason, which is recorded in the job's Activity log as a workflow status change. Delivered phases are left as they are; add or update phases, then sign the scope off again as normal. It deliberately goes to Scoping rather than In Progress: with every existing phase still finished, the auto-complete task would otherwise close the job again straight away.

    New phases can't be added to (or cloned onto) a job that is Completed, Lost, Deleted or Archived, or before scoping has started — reopen it first. This stops new work hiding behind a Completed job, where it wouldn't appear on the dashboard or in the QA and delivery queues.

    As a safety net, if a phase that's in flight (Pending Scheduling up to Completed) ever ends up on a Completed job, saving it moves the job back to In Progress and logs why. Old never-started Draft phases on completed jobs are ignored.

    Reopening a phase (see Phase lifecycle) also reopens its job to In Progress if the job was Completed.

    One-off cleanup (migration 0088)¶

    Existing data from before these guards was tidied once on upgrade. On each Completed job:

    • an in-flight phase with activity in the last 90 days (a booking, or created / status changed) reopens the job to In Progress;
    • older in-flight phases, and Draft phases that were never started, are moved to Archived.

    Each change is logged in the Activity log as a workflow status change by SYSTEM, with "data cleanup" as the reason.

    Once a job is Lost, Deleted or Archived, its phases can't move forward through the workflow, can't take new scheduler bookings, and are ignored by the automatic workflow and late-notification tasks. A Completed job's schedule is locked too — see Booking & Validation.
