    Dashboard Alarms¶

    The dashboard's Alarms tab lists phases and jobs that need attention. What you see depends on your role: unit managers see every alarm in their units, TQA / PQA reviewers see QA alarms unit-wide, schedulers see scheduling alarms unit-wide, and everyone sees alarms for work they're on (booked, lead, report author, or on the job's account/scoping team).

    There are two groups. The red count on the tab is overdue only; stale items have their own grey count.

    Overdue¶

    Live work that's late or missing something:

    Alarm Means
    Delivery overdue The phase's wanted/scheduled delivery date has passed.
    Tech QA / Pres QA overdue The phase is past its TQA / PQA due date and still before / in that QA stage.
    No Tech QA / Pres QA In (or waiting for) QA with no reviewer assigned.
    Unscheduled Marked as scheduled (or later) but with no bookings.
    No billing code Booked work with no billing code to charge it to.
    Job overdue The job's delivery date has passed.

    Stale¶

    Work that has stopped moving. These are left out of the overdue lists so those only show genuinely late work, and nobody gets daily late reminders for them — someone needs to pick them back up or close them off.

    Alarm Means Usually
    Stalled phases Schedule Tentative to In Progress, with no status change for STALE_PHASE_DAYS and nothing booked since. Move it on (e.g. to QA), postpone or cancel it.
    Jobs with no live phases Scope signed off or later, every phase finished, but the job can't complete — usually all phases were cancelled. Close the job (Lost before it's In Progress, otherwise Delete) or add the missing work.
    Never booked Pending Scheduling with the wanted delivery date more than STALE_PHASE_DAYS ago and nothing ever booked. Re-date, postpone or cancel it.
    Long postponed Postponed for more than STALE_POSTPONED_DAYS (a postponed phase stops its job completing). Bring it back to Pending Scheduling or cancel it.
    Dormant jobs Draft, scoping or sign-off with no status change for STALE_JOB_DAYS. Move it on or close it as Lost.

    QA and awaiting-delivery phases never count as stalled: waiting on a reviewer with nothing booked is normal there, so they stay in the overdue alarms and keep their reminders.

    The thresholds are on Settings → Stale Alarms (see CHAOTICA settings).

    Related behaviour¶

    • Starting a phase moves its job to In Progress from either Pending Start or Scoping Complete, so jobs can't get stuck short of auto-completing.
    • A job only completes (automatically or by hand) if at least one phase was delivered. A job whose phases were all cancelled delivered nothing; it shows under Jobs with no live phases instead.
